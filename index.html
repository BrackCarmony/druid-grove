Druid Grove

The moon broke and the whole planet went grey. You are the last wood nymph, with a pocketful of seeds and a pair of tweezers. When your satchel runs dry, head back to the glowing Seed Hollow or any waystone you have raised.

Pick a world to play. Click captures the mouse; Esc releases it.

Settings

Render distance

Lower the render distance if the game stutters: it draws less of the planet, flowers and trees far away. M mutes all sound in game.
